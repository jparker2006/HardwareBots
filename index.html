journeyman_

It learns a job by watching an expert once.
Then it teaches the next person.

Gemma 4 · NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor · a camera, a mic, no cloud

Like you're five

1 · WATCH

The expert shows it

The expert does the job and talks. Journeyman sees through a camera and hears through a mic.

2 · REMEMBER

It writes it down

Gemma turns what it saw and heard into numbered steps. If a step has no reason, it asks "why?"

3 · TEACH

It teaches the new person

The new person says "teach me." It reads each step and checks with the camera. If it doesn't know an answer, it says so and saves the question for the expert.

Talk track

WHY COOLIt asks "why?", checks your work with a camera, and says "I don't know" instead of making things up. No cloud.
